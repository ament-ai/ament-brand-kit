04 · Usage

Leave it alone, give it room

Clear space equals the height of the wordmark on every side. Smallest sizes: mark 24px (16px flat or mono), wordmark 9px tall, horizontal lockup 120px wide.

Do
  • Use the supplied files and scale them proportionally.
  • Use the colour mark on graphite or calm dark surfaces.
  • Use the mono ink mark on light and busy backgrounds.
  • Write "AMENT Hive" first, then "Hive".
Don't
  • Recolour, stretch, rotate or re-proportion the logo.
  • Add shadows, glows, outlines, bevels or 3D effects.
  • Retype "AMENT" in a font or add a crossbar to the A.
  • Use the retired honey-yellow hexagon.
