# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-16)

First public release of the AMENT brand kit.

- **Logos:** vector mark (colour, flat colour, mono ink/black/white), wordmark (champagne, champagne-deep, ink,
  black, white), horizontal and stacked lockups, and the AMENT Hive lockup, on dark and light. The geometry
  matches ament.ai and AMENT Hive.
- **PNG exports** of every logo at 256, 512, 1024 and 2048px wide, with transparent backgrounds.
- **Concept renders:** the original raster logo concepts in `logos/source/`.
- **App icons:** `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), Apple touch icon (180), PWA icons (192/512) and a
  maskable icon (512).
- **Colour:** design tokens (`tokens.json`, DTCG format), CSS custom properties, a Tailwind v4 `@theme` block,
  Adobe `.ase` and GIMP `.gpl` palettes, and a swatch sheet.
- **Typography:** Archivo and IBM Plex Mono usage, embed snippets and type scale.
- **Guidelines:** identity, logo usage and misuse, colour proportions, shape and motion, product naming,
  and voice and tone in English and Turkish.
- **index.html:** a one-page brand overview.
